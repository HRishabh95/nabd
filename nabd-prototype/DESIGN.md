# Nabd — design system (v3, institutional)

Mode: Operate. Use scene: CBUAE executives and analysts, daytime offices and a meeting-room screen. Light.
Reference bar: institutional finance software, calm and serious. Mubadala/DGE screenshots were not available; match them when they are.

- Identity: the CBUAE emblem (assets/cbuae-logo.png, transparent) on a white top bar, a divider, then the product name Nabd with Arabic نبض.
- Palette, taken from the emblem:
  - Falcon bronze #8C6A3F is the brand colour: active navigation, data lines, uncertainty bands (tints #F3ECE2, #E2D3BC, #C9AE86), entry-card hover edge. Bronze text uses #6E5230.
  - Charcoal #16181B is the ink and the primary button.
  - UAE flag green #0B7A3E and ribbon red #B3122B carry meaning only: better and worse.
  - Neutral cool ground #F4F5F7, white panels, 1px #DFE2E6 borders. No cream, no gradients, no shadows at rest.
- Type: Source Sans 3 for everything (tabular figures on), Noto Sans Arabic for Arabic. Sizes 13 / 14 / 15 / 18 / 30, figures 26 to 40.
- Layout: content max 1312px, 12 columns, 24px gutters, 8px spacing base.
- Navigation: standard top bar. Home, Scenarios, Nowcast, Time machine | Network, Fraud, Governance. A "Viewing as" role switcher applies the information barrier live.
- Uncertainty is always drawn: fans and bands, never a single line alone.
